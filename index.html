About this tool: Prices are theoretical fair values computed under the Black-Scholes (1973) and Cox-Ross-Rubinstein (1979) models, assuming lognormal returns, constant volatility, and no arbitrage. Real market prices will differ due to implied volatility skew, liquidity, early exercise premium, and bid-ask spreads. Not financial advice. Always verify with a live data source before trading.
